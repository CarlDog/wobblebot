# 2.0.12 fill-trade recovery patch (ADR-046)

This patch closes the silent fill-loss path found on 2026-09-18: a fill the
exchange had confirmed whose trade rows the same tick's `TradesHistory`
snapshot did not yet list was committed as a closed order with zero trade
rows, and nothing re-examined it. A confirmed fill whose rows do not cover
its `filled_amount` is now pending, never final: the order is closed and a
`pending_fill_trades` marker is written in one transaction, the engine sweeps
the marker every tick using the order's own trade list (`QueryOrders
trades=true`, then `QueryTrades`), and it gives up loudly after a bound. The
roadmap owns the incident receipt, the review gate and the deployment status;
`docs/architecture/decisions.md` (ADR-046) owns the design.

## Configuration and data compatibility

No settings, prompt, provider-key or environment change. Keep the operator's
current `settings.yml`.

`live.db` gains one additive table, `pending_fill_trades`, created by the
writable `connect()` on the first boot of `cli/live`; read-only openers skip
schema by design and are unaffected. The completeness critic exercised the
upgrade against a 2.0.11-shape database: exactly that table is added, nothing
is dropped, `integrity_check` is ok before and after, and a second open is
idempotent. No existing row changes.

The Kraken trader key needs no new permission: `QueryOrders` and
`QueryTrades` sit under the existing "query open & closed orders & trades"
scope, and both calls were verified read-only against the live account before
the tag.

## What the operator will see

- A `grid fill` log line at WARNING with `recovery scheduled` while a fill's
  rows are owed, followed by `fill fully recorded` (INFO) with the lookup
  count; an ordinary fill stays INFO. A high lookup count means the order's
  own trade list is not leading `TradesHistory` and the fast path is not fast.
- A new critical notification, "Fill recorded without its trade rows", if
  the sweep gives up (120 lookups that ran and left the fill uncovered, or 30
  minutes). The marker is kept, re-raised at ERROR on every later boot until
  the rows are backfilled, and cleared once they are. The daily reconcile
  reports the same gap only once Kraken's history lists the trade, and only
  for symbols in `live.symbols`.
- At boot, a WARNING naming the symbols whose owed rows this session will
  sweep, and an ERROR for markers on symbols no longer in `live.symbols`
  (nothing sweeps or reconciles those; backfill or add the symbol back).
- The ADR-038 fee-drift page can now fire on a recovery tick, not only on a
  fill tick.

## Rollout and rollback

Deploy is the usual two operator steps and neither is automatic: the `v2.0.12`
tag builds and publishes the image; an `IMAGE_TAG` bump on the file-based
stack applies it. Before recreating `wobblebot-live`, confirm no fill is
mid-recovery: a `recovery scheduled` WARNING without a matching `fill fully
recorded` means wait for it. After the bump, confirm the boot line and that
`pending_fill_trades` exists.

Rolling back to 2.0.11 leaves the table in place, which 2.0.11 ignores. Any
marker written by 2.0.12 is then unswept; run
`tools/reconcile_trade_history.py --symbols <traded>` after a rollback and
follow its backfill runbook for any reported gap. Backfilled rows must carry
Kraken's `ordertxid` as `order_id` for a later 2.0.12 boot to clear the marker.

## Open items carried, none blocking

Whether `QueryTrades` answers a not-yet-indexed trade id with an empty result
(a counted attempt) or an error envelope (an uncounted transport failure) is
unverified; either shape is bounded by the 30-minute ceiling. A probe script
is staged on the NAS volume. The account's Kraken counter tier is unmeasured;
markers appearing in the same tick look up on the same third tick. The full
list is in `docs/release/v2.0-known-limitations.md` and the PR
(https://github.com/CarlDog/wobblebot/pull/152).
